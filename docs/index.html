How to install

  1. Download it on your phone Tap the button above in Chrome. The file is called Reclaim-beta.apk.
  2. Open the file and allow the install Android will ask you to allow your browser to install unknown apps. Turn it on for this one install.
    If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway. This is a beta that is not on the Play Store yet.
  3. Open Reclaim and follow the setup It takes about a minute: allow two permissions, pick at least 3 interests, choose the apps to limit, and set your daily limit.
  4. Turn on the two permissions Usage Access lets Reclaim add up your time. Accessibility lets it notice when one of your chosen apps opens.
    If the Accessibility switch is greyed out (Android 13 and newer): open your phone's Settings › Apps › Reclaim, tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner, choose Allow restricted settings, then turn Reclaim on under Accessibility.
    Keep it switched on: don't swipe Reclaim away from your recent apps and don't use "clear all". On vivo, iQOO, Xiaomi and Oppo phones that switches Accessibility off, and Reclaim stops blocking. Lock Reclaim in the recent-apps screen to protect it. If it ever turns off, Reclaim's Home screen shows a "Blocking is off" warning with a button to turn it back on.

What it does

  • One combined limit. Pick the apps that pull you in and give them a single daily budget, from 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  • A kind heads-up. A few minutes before your time is up, a small note tells you how much you have used.
  • Something better to do. When time is up, opening one of those apps shows a calm breathing pause and articles picked from your interests.
  • A limit you can't change in a weak moment. Once set, the limit stays for 30 days.

Your privacy

Everything stays on your phone. Reclaim has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own. Your limit, your apps and your usage never leave your device.

The Accessibility permission is used only to see which app is opening. It never reads your screen, messages or anything you type.

The only internet use is fetching articles from public news feeds. Read the full privacy policy.
