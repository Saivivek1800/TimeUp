Reclaim

Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Reclaim Android app

In short: Reclaim has no account, no ads and no analytics. It does not collect your personal data and it has no server to send it to. What you set up in the app stays on your phone.

What Reclaim does

Reclaim helps you spend less time on social media. You choose some apps and one daily time limit. When the limit is used up, opening one of those apps shows a reading screen with articles instead.

What stays on your phone

To work, Reclaim saves a few things only on your device:

  • Your daily limit, the apps you chose to limit, your interests and your theme choice.
  • How many minutes you used the chosen apps each day (to show your progress and your streak).
  • Articles it has downloaded, and which ones you have read, so the list works offline.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Reclaim turns off Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data. To erase everything, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings or uninstall the app.

The permissions it asks for, and why

PermissionWhat it is used for
Usage AccessTo add up how long you spend in the apps you chose, so it can tell when your daily limit is reached. This is read on your phone and stays there.
Accessibility ServiceTo notice which app has just opened, so it can compare it with your list and show the reading screen once your limit is used up. Reclaim reads only the app's package name. It does not read what is on your screen, your messages, passwords or anything you type, and it does not tap, type or change anything in other apps. The service is configured so that it cannot read screen content at all.
InternetTo download articles from public news feeds and their pictures.

You can turn off either special permission at any time in your phone's settings. Reclaim then cannot limit your apps until you turn it back on.

Articles and other websites

Articles are fetched from the public feeds of websites such as news and magazine sites. When your phone downloads a feed or a picture, that website can see your phone's internet address (IP address) and standard request details, just as when you visit any web page. Reclaim does not add any identifier about you. When you open an article, it opens in your browser (a Chrome Custom Tab) and that website's own privacy policy applies from then on.

What Reclaim does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or messages.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because there is no data to share.

Children

Reclaim is not made for children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If a change affects what the app does with your data, the app will tell you.
